What to buy for your daughter's first season.

The rulebook list first. Then what to ask before you buy.

CCCraig Clayton · Author of Girls Flag Football, Illustrated6 min read2026–27 NFHS rules

In this post

The short answer
A mouthguard, shoes and a belt.

Every player must wear a mouth protector, a tucked-in jersey, shorts or pants with no pockets, shoes, and a flag belt with three flags. Everything else is optional. 1-5-1

Must
01

What the rulebook requires

  • 1Mouth protector. One tooth and mouth protector, properly fitted. 1-5-1c
  • 2Jersey. Unaltered, tucked in, with a number on the front and back.
  • 3Shorts, pants or skirt. No pockets, no exposed drawstrings, no belts or belt loops, and one solid color that clearly contrasts with the flags.
  • 4Shoes. They have to cover the foot and have a firm sole. Cleats are allowed, and so are shoes with no cleats.
  • 5Flag belt. One solid color, three flags, one on each hip and one on the tailbone. 1-5-1f
Ask
02

Ask before you buy

Jerseys and numbers usually come from the team. Belts are worth a question too, because the state can set the style of flag. Ask the coach what the school hands out, so you don't buy something she can't use.

The one thing you almost always buy yourself is shoes. A mouth protector is next. High school rules say a mouth protector can't have attachments that don't protect the teeth or mouth. They don't say whether a strap counts, so ask your coach before you buy a strapped one.

Cleats
03

Cleats, in plain words

The rulebook doesn't require cleats. If she wears them, non-removable cleats can't be longer than ½ inch and have to be made of nonabrasive rubber or a similar material that won't develop a cutting edge.

Many flag players wear molded, soccer-style cleats. I can't tell you a specific pair is legal, because the NFHS doesn't test or approve equipment. If you're unsure, bring them to the coach.

Extras
04

Nice to have, not required

The rules allow soft extras: knee pads at least ½ inch thick, hip and tailbone pads, forearm pads, soft headwear, and elastic or cloth hair bands. Gloves need a NOCSAE or SFIA seal on the wrist, unless they're plain cloth.

A water bottle and a drawstring bag make a long game day easier. Skip jewelry, which is illegal equipment, and shoulder pads, which aren't allowed.
