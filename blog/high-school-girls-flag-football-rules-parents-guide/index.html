High school girls flag football rules, on one page.

What a parent needs to know before the first game.

CCCraig Clayton · Author of Girls Flag Football, Illustrated5 min read2026–27 NFHS rules

In this post

The short answer
Seven players, 48 minutes, no contact.

Teams start with seven players, play four 12-minute periods, and score with touchdowns, tries and safeties. Pulling the flag ends the play, and contact is a foul. 1-1-3 · 3-1-1

Game
01

The game in five lines

  • 1Seven players per team start the game. A team can play with as few as five. 1-1-3
  • 2Four downs to reach the next zone line. Get there and you earn a new series.
  • 3Pull the flag and the play is over. When the ball carrier's flag comes off, her down ends.
  • 4No tackling and no contact blocking. Intentional contact by either team is a foul.
  • 5No kickoffs. Each half starts with a snap from your own 14-yard line.
48
02

The clock

A game is 48 minutes of clock time. That's four 12-minute periods, one minute between periods, and a halftime of 5 to 20 minutes that your state sets. 3-1-1

The offense gets a 25-second play clock. Miss it and it's a 5-yard delay of game. Each team gets three charged time-outs per half. 3-5-1

Score
03

How a team scores

  • 1Touchdown: 6 points.
  • 2Try after a touchdown: 2 points from the 10-yard line, or 1 point from the 3-yard line.
  • 3Safety: 2 points for the other team.
  • 4Field goal: 3 points, but only where your state association allows it.

More on every way to score in how scoring works.

State
04

What your state decides

The NFHS writes the rules, but each state association chooses some options. Field size, field goals, halftime length, how ties are settled and whether a mercy rule applies can all differ.

“Always check your state association's adoptions.”

Ask the coach, or look on your state association's website, for the choices your league made.
